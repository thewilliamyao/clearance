The Index

Prints filed by Recorders on shift 5 of Clearance, sent from their units. Each one is kept only as a 40×30 two-tone print, the label it was filed under and the day it arrived.

CONTACTING THE INDEX…
▸ PLAY CLEARANCE

Nothing else is kept: no photo, no sharper print, no location, nothing that identifies a unit or the Recorder who sent it. A unit sends prints only when its Recorder turns on SHARE WITH THE INDEX, and Recorders are asked to share only people who agreed to be photographed.

To have a print taken down, write to hello@thewilliamyao.com with its number.
